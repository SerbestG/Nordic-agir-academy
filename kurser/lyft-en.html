Who is this course for?

For those attaching loads, acting as signaller or supervising work where lifting takes place. The course meets the Swedish work environment requirement for documented knowledge. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Choose the right lifting equipment and inspect it before use
  • Assess weight, centre of gravity and risks before the lift
  • Signal and communicate safely with the crane operator
  • Know the responsibilities under Swedish work environment rules

Course content

Rules and responsibilityThe Swedish Work Environment Act and the AFS requirements — who is responsible for what during a lift.
Lifting equipment and inspectionSlings, chains, lifting beams — markings, discard criteria and daily checks.
Planning the liftWeight, centre of gravity, angles and surroundings. The three-minute risk assessment that saves lives.
Attaching and signallingSafe attachment methods and standardised hand signals for communicating with the operator.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
