Who is this course for?

For contract engineers, estimators, site managers and bid managers — everyone who meets the AF part in tender documents and contracts. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Understand the AF part’s role in the AMA family and the ranking of documents
  • Read and interpret the provisions on bids, time, liability and economy
  • Recognise penalties, insurance requirements and securities before they cost you
  • Use the AF part actively in bid work and project control

Course content

The AF part’s place in the systemAMA AF, the ranking against AB/ABT and why the AF part is read BEFORE the quantities.
The bid provisionsFormal requirements, deadlines, references and exclusion grounds — what disqualifies bids unnecessarily.
The contract provisionsTime, penalties, insurance, securities and inspections — the economy between the lines.
Meetings, organisation and communicationSite meetings, notification paths and diary requirements governed by the AF part.
AF in daily workFrom bid review to close-out — the checklists that turn the AF part into a tool.
