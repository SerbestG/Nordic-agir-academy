Who is this course for?

For new and aspiring construction project managers — and experienced ones who want structure in the role: contract engineers, supervisors on the way up and client-side project managers. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Understand the project manager’s assignment, mandate and interfaces with the site manager and the client
  • Lead the project through its phases: start-up, production, close-out
  • Control economy, time and changes with the right tools and meeting culture
  • Lead people: the team, the conflicts and the communication that decides

Course content

The role and the mandateWhat a construction project manager actually owns — and the boundaries towards site manager, client and estimating.
The project startAssignment review, organisation, contract review and the kick-off meeting that sets the tone.
Control during productionFinancial and schedule follow-up, change management and everyday decision logic.
The peopleThe team, subcontractor relations, the conflict ladder and communication up and down.
The close-outInspection, handover, lessons learned — and the art of finishing profitably.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
