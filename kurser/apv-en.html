Who is this course for?

For everyone working on or beside roads in Sweden where Trafikverket sets competence requirements. The course covers all of APV Step 1 — competences 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 — and provides the documented basic competence required to be present and work on a road work site. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Meet Trafikverket’s requirements for general basic competence (step 1.1)
  • Know the requirements for drivers of road maintenance vehicles (step 1.2)
  • Work safely with road works close to passing traffic (step 1.3)
  • Understand your role, your responsibility and the risks on the road work site

Course content

The road as a workplaceThe risk picture, the accident statistics and why the rules exist — for your safety and the road users’.
Step 1.1 — General basic competenceThe essentials everyone on site must know: the rules, protective arrangements, zoning and personal protective equipment.
Step 1.2 — Drivers of road maintenance vehiclesThe requirements for those driving maintenance, service and works vehicles on and beside the road.
Step 1.3 — Road works close to trafficFor those carrying out the work itself: safe working methods next to passing traffic and correct temporary signage.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
