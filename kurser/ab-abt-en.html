Who is this course for?

For those working in Swedish construction or design-and-build contracts who want to truly know the standard contracts — not just know they exist in the back pocket. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Tell the difference between construction and design-and-build liability
  • Handle notifications, obstacles and extensions of time correctly
  • Understand the financial regulation and the payment flow
  • Be confident going into inspection and the warranty period
