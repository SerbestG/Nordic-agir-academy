Who is this course for?

For those who hold the design together — design managers, project managers with design responsibility and consultants coordinating other consultants. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Plan the design work with the right stages and decision points
  • Lead the consultant team and manage the interfaces between disciplines
  • Review documents efficiently and secure their quality
  • Keep schedule and budget when the input changes

Course content

The design manager’s assignmentThe role, the responsibility and the relationship with the client, the project manager and the consultant team.
Planning the designStages, deliverables, decision points and a schedule that survives reality.
Leading the consultant teamKick-off, design meetings, responsibility matrices and coordination duties.
Review and qualityReview routines, clash detection and catching the errors before they are built.
Economy and changesThe design budget, change management and ABK 09 in everyday practice.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
