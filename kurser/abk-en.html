Who is this course for?

For consultants, consultant brokers and buyers of technical services — everyone who writes or signs consulting agreements and wants to know what they actually mean. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Define the engagement so both parties know what is included
  • Understand the consultant’s liability and how it is limited
  • Set the right insurance requirements — and meet them
  • Know who owns the result and may use it
