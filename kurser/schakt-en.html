Who is this course for?

For skilled workers, machine operators, supervisors and site managers — and whoever is appointed excavation supervisor on site. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Understand how soils behave and why trench collapses kill
  • Plan the excavation: ground conditions, utilities, loads, slope or support
  • Carry out the excavation supervisor’s daily checks and know the stop criteria
  • Write the rescue plan that saves lives — before anyone goes down

Course content

Why trench collapses killA cubic metre of soil weighs as much as a car — the maths, the timelines and the situations where it happens.
Soil: the material that sets the rulesGranular soils, clay, silt and till — and water, the main enemy of stability.
Roles, responsibility and the excavation supervisorClient, BAS-P/U, employer — and the role description that gives real authority to stop work.
Planning and choice of methodGeotechnics, utility location (Ledningskollen), safety distances — slopes when there is room, trench boxes and sheet piling when there is not.
Daily operation and rescueThe inspection round, the stop signals and the rescue plan — why impulsive rescue creates more victims.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
