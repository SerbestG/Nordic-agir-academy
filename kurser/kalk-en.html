Who is this course for?

For estimators, contract engineers, site managers and business owners who price jobs — and want to win them with the margin intact. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Go from tender documents to a complete estimate with a method
  • Calculate labour, materials, machinery and subcontractors correctly
  • Set mark-ups that cover risk and overheads
  • Quality-assure the estimate before the bid is submitted

The estimate’s role in the bidFrom enquiry to bid sum — the process and the decisions along the way.
