Who is this course for?

For estimators, site managers, contract engineers and operations managers in civil engineering companies who price and settle work against a bill of quantities. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Read the bill of quantities: codes, units, fixed and adjustable quantities
  • Apply the principle of theoretical quantity to excavation, fill and trenches
  • Separate quantity adjustment from variations — and document to get paid
  • Price bids on a bill of quantities with conscious risk assessment
