Who is this course for?

For site managers, supervisors, contract engineers and project managers — everyone who runs into variations and additional work in daily operations and wants to stop working for free. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Recognise ÄTA work (variations) when it appears — including the deemed ones
  • Notify on time, in the right way, to the right person
  • Document so the claim holds even if it ends in a dispute
  • Price and settle variations without unnecessary conflict

Course content

What is ÄTA work, really?Alterations, additions and omissions under AB 04/ABT 06 — and the deemed variations many miss.
The duty to notifyWhen, how and to whom. The formal requirements that decide whether you get paid or not.
Documentation that holdsSite diary, photos, e-mails and meeting minutes — building your evidence while the work is ongoing.
Pricing and settlementUnit prices, cost-plus and mark-ups. How to calculate and how to negotiate.
When the discussion comes anywayStrategies in disagreement — from site meeting to dispute resolution.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
