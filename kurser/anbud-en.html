Who is this course for?

For those who write or review bids in Swedish public procurement — bid managers, estimators and business owners who want higher hit rates and fewer unnecessary rejections. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Read the tender documents systematically and find every mandatory requirement
  • Build a requirement matrix that ensures nothing is missed
  • Write answers the evaluator understands and can score
  • Final-check the bid before submission — formalities, attachments and evidence

Course content

The tender documents under the magnifierThe structure of the documents, mandatory requirements versus award criteria — and reading like a procurement officer.
The requirement matrixBuild a simple matrix linking every requirement to an answer, evidence and a responsible person.
Write for the evaluatorClear answers, the right references and attachments that prove what you promise — without padding.
The final reviewThe pre-submission checklist: formalities, signatures and the most common disqualification mistakes.
Final examOnline knowledge test with unlimited attempts. Certificate in English.
