Who is this course for?

For foreign professionals and companies working on Swedish construction sites: site managers, supervisors, designers and coordinators who need to understand the Swedish BAS-P/BAS-U system. The course is held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Understand the Swedish Work Environment Act and the client’s duties
  • Know the roles: client, BAS-P (planning) and BAS-U (execution)
  • Apply the work environment plan (arbetsmiljöplan) in practice
  • Coordinate contractors safely — and document it correctly
