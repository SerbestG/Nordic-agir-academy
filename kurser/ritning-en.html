Who is this course for?

For site managers, supervisors, skilled workers and clients — everyone who needs to understand what the drawing actually says before it is built. Held entirely in English.

After the course you will be able to

  • Read plans, sections, elevations and details with a correct sense of scale
  • Interpret symbols, line types, dimensioning and references
  • Keep track of drawing numbers, revisions and the current document
  • Connect the drawing to the specification and quantities — the whole that governs the build

Course content

The language of drawingsScales, line types, symbols and dimensioning — the grammar everything rests on.
The drawing typesSite plan, floor plans, sections, elevations, details and schedules — what each type answers.
Disciplines and coordinationArchitectural, structural and services drawings — and how to read them together.
