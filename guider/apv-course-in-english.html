Working on Swedish roads: the APV course in English

To work on or beside roads where Trafikverket (the Swedish Transport Administration) sets competence requirements, you need documented basic competence: APV Step 1. Our online course covers all of Step 1 entirely in English for 995 SEK excl. VAT.

What is APV Step 1?

APV (Arbete på väg — work on roads) is Trafikverket's competence requirement for road work sites. Step 1 is the basic level and has three parts:

PartFor whom
1.1 General basic competenceEveryone present and working on the road work site.
1.2 Drivers of road maintenance vehiclesThose driving maintenance, service or works vehicles on and beside the road.
1.3 Road works close to trafficThose carrying out the work next to passing traffic, including temporary signage.

Does it apply to all roads?

The requirements come from Trafikverket and apply to their assignments. Municipalities and other road authorities may set similar requirements in their contracts. Always ask your client which competence the assignment requires.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs APV training in Sweden?

Everyone working on or beside roads where Trafikverket sets competence requirements. Which part of Step 1 applies depends on your role.

Can I take APV Step 1 in English?

Yes. Our course is held entirely in English and online, for 995 SEK excl. VAT.

Do I need all three parts?

It depends on your role and your client. Our course covers all three, so you are covered either way.

How long is the certificate valid?

Our certificate is valid for five years from the exam date and has a QR code for verification.
